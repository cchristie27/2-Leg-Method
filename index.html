How this is graded

Both legs have to land outright — no pushes, no partial credit. Grading uses the official final result, overtime and extra innings included.

Staking is flat: half a unit ($50) on the parlay, one full unit ($100) on each single. Every price shown is the American odds at the time the bet was placed.

This is a log of what I bet. Subscriptions buy access to the plays, not a result — nothing here is guaranteed and past performance doesn't predict future returns. The sample is small, a couple of tickets carry most of the return, and a run like this says very little about what comes next.
